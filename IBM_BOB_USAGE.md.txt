# IBM Bob Technology Usage - GreenWave AI

**Project Name:** GreenWave AI: Intelligent Carbon Document Parser for Supply Chains  
**Core Tool:** IBM Bob (AI-Powered Development Assistant)

---

## How IBM Bob Was Used in Development

IBM Bob served as our primary AI collaboration partner throughout the lifecycle of building the GreenWave AI proof-of-concept.

### 1. Specification & Data Architecture
* **Prompt Engineering:** Defined the extraction schema for unstructured PDF invoices and shipping receipts.
* **Scope Definition:** Defined extraction parameters targeting Scope 3 greenhouse gas (CO2e) emissions from transport metrics (weight, distance).

### 2. Code Generation & Parser Logic
* **Python Pipeline:** Drafted the core parsing logic (`parser.py`) to process raw invoice text (`invoice.txt`) and output structured JSON data.
* **Automated Calculations:** Coded calculation rules based on heavy-freight emissions factors (0.105 kg CO2e / ton-km).
